Project Overview

Over the past few weeks, we have been looking at the differences between JMU and other college campuses. During this project, we focused on the differences between Virginia Tech and JMU. We looked at the differences in community relationships between Virginia Tech's Reddit comments and James Madison's Reddit comments. While looking through Virginia Tech's Reddit comments, we found that Virginia Tech talks about downtown Blacksburg, visits more downtown places and bars, and is more outside community-centered than James Madison is. We found that many students view Virginia Tech as a part of Blacksburg, rather than at JMU, where Harrisonburg is viewed as a part of the university.
